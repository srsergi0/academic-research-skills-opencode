# HEI Domain Glossary

Spain Higher Education domain-specific terminology. Used by all agents when writing papers in the HEI field.

## Institutional Types

| English | Español | Notes |
|---------|---------|-------|
| Higher Education Institution (HEI) | Institución de Educación Superior (IES) | General term for universities and centres |
| Public University | Universidad pública | 50 in Spain (2024) |
| Private University | Universidad privada | ~40 in Spain (2024) |
| University System | Sistema Universitario Español (SUE) | Coordinated by the Ministry and the regions |

## Quality Assurance

| English | Español | Notes |
|---------|---------|-------|
| Accreditation | Acreditación | Ex-ante programme assessment (ANECA/regional agencies) |
| Quality Assurance | Garantía de calidad | Internal quality assurance systems (SGIC) |
| Monitoring / Follow-up | Seguimiento | Periodic programme monitoring reports |
| Programme Modification | Modificación | Substantial vs non-substantial changes |
| Institutional Accreditation | Acreditación institucional | Centre-level accreditation |
| Teaching Assessment | Evaluación docente | DOCENTIA programme and equivalents |

## Research Evaluation

| English | Español | Notes |
|---------|---------|-------|
| Six-year Research Period | Sexenio | CNEAI research assessment (6-year periods) |
| Five-year Teaching Period | Quinquenio | Teaching merits component |
| ANECA | ANECA | Agencia Nacional de Evaluación y Acreditación |
| AEI | AEI | Agencia Estatal de Investigación (ROR 10.13039/501100011033) |
| Accreditation for Tenure | Acreditación (Titular / Catedrático) | PEP and ACADEMIA programmes |

## Current Issues

| English | Español | Notes |
|---------|---------|-------|
| Declining Birthrate | Descenso de la natalidad / invierno demográfico | Key issue for enrolment planning |
| Dropout | Abandono | Tasa de abandono (SIIU indicator) |
| Inbound Mobility | Movilidad entrante | International students coming to Spain |
| Performance Rate | Tasa de rendimiento | SIIU academic performance indicator |
